- `nice shot :gg:` → nice shot gg ᕦ(ò_óˇ)ᕤ

- `so happy :excited:` → so happy \(^o^)/
- `@amikiir :love:` → @amikiir (♥‿♥) with yellow highlight on @amikiir

## Total: 150+ Kaomoji & Emoticons Available!

You can combine kaomoji with:
- @mentions (they'll be highlighted in yellow)
- Rich text tags like `<b>bold</b>`, `<i>italic</i>`, `<color=#FF0000>red</color>`
- Multiple kaomoji in one message!
